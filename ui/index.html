About Google sign-in

The release installer comes preconfigured. You only need to click Sign in with Google; no Google Cloud setup is needed by users.

If this preview says sign-in is unavailable, its developer has not supplied the app's Google Desktop OAuth client yet. Ask for a configured release build. Renting and sharing remain locked without Google sign-in.

Sign-in opens your system browser. Your verified name and email are shared with your trusted coordinator. Passwords and Google access tokens are never stored. The allowance is keyed to your Google account, not your device. There is no public host discovery service yet.
